What the full evaluation found

Real signal: UCC 4G LTE drive-test dataset (Raca, Quinlan, Zahran and Sreenan, ACM MMSys 2018, CC BY 4.0). Basement entries and look-alikes: SANKET physics simulator calibrated on that dataset.

The detector here is the JavaScript port of the Python reference, checked tick by tick against it (0 mismatches over 1,737 test ticks).

The same detector, in Java, runs in the SANKET Android app, which protects real downloads and replays this ramp trip as its demo. Free test build: github.com/c0ldsheep/SANKET-app. Website: c0ldsheep.github.io/SANKET-app.
